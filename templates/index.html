About Us

WheyOnWheels is a Mumbai-based protein nutrition brand supplying premium premix blends to corporate cafeterias and college canteens across the city. We work with rising whey brands like Avvatar to deliver affordable, high-quality, and great-tasting protein smoothies, creatine drinks, and nutritious snacks - making clean nutrition accessible to students and professionals alike. Our premix solutions are designed for convenience, allowing canteens and cafeterias to serve consistent, performance-focused nutrition with zero hassle. Currently in our beta phase, we're using this canteen and corporate supply model to refine our blends, understand our customers, and build a loyal base. Next up - our very own food truck hitting the streets of Mumbai, bringing WheyOnWheels directly to fitness enthusiasts citywide. And that's just the beginning. Our long-term vision is a full FMCG pivot, putting WheyOnWheels products on shelves across India.

Powered by Avvatar Whey Protein

Avvatar is India's first 100% whey protein made from pure cow's milk — sourced, processed, and packaged entirely within the country by Parag Milk Foods. With international-grade quality and unmatched freshness, Avvatar delivers complete protein with a high biological value and rich amino acid profile.

Every scoop is free from preservatives, added sugar, and banned substances, making it a trusted choice for athletes and fitness enthusiasts across India. At WheyOnWheels, we proudly blend every smoothie with Avvatar Whey to ensure clean, authentic, and effective nutrition — straight from India's own protein powerhouse.

What Is Whey Protein?

Whey protein is a high-quality, fast-absorbing protein that helps your body repair and build muscle. It contains essential amino acids that support recovery and overall fitness.

Why Is It Important?

  • Boosts muscle growth and repair
  • Speeds up recovery after workouts
  • Keeps you full and reduces cravings

How Does It Work?

Rich in BCAAs, whey protein triggers muscle protein synthesis — the process your body uses to build new muscle tissue. Its fast absorption makes it ideal post-workout for maximum results.

What Are Creatine Drinks?

Creatine drinks are flavoured beverages infused with creatine monohydrate — one of the most researched and effective supplements for improving strength, power, and performance.

Why Are They Important?

  • Increase strength and explosive power
  • Enhance muscle endurance during high-intensity training
  • Support faster muscle recovery between sets
  • Help build lean muscle mass over time

How Do They Work?

Creatine increases phosphocreatine stores in your muscles, allowing your body to produce more ATP — the primary energy source for short bursts of intense activity. Our flavoured creatine drinks make it easy and enjoyable to hit your daily dose consistently.
